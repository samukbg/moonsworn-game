Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Moonsworn is a game made by Samuel Gomes ("I", "me"). This policy covers the Moonsworn apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, macOS and Windows. In short: Moonsworn does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • Nothing is sold to or shared with third parties.

What stays on your device

Your settings, progress, best scores and a record of what you own (so purchases work without a connection) are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent to me. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I never see your name, payment details or account. The game only receives a confirmation from the store of which items your account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Online co-op

When you host or join an online game, your device connects directly to your friends' devices. To do that, the devices exchange network (IP) addresses and the game's inputs and positions, only for as long as the game lasts. There is no Moonsworn server, no chat and no voice, and nothing is recorded or kept. The host's address is remembered on your device so you can rejoin it.

Children

Moonsworn collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: samuelbg04@gmail.com.
